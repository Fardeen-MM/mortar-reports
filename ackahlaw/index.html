01
Optimize Ads — Google + Relaunch Meta
The Problem: Your Google Ads are running but likely under-optimized. Meta was abandoned after a failed test — but the platform isn't broken, the strategy was.
🛠️ DIY Fix
1Audit current Google Ads: review search terms report, pause irrelevant keywords
2Restructure ad groups by intent: "corporate immigration," "work permit," "LMIA," "spousal sponsorship"
3Write 3 new ad variations per group — highlight flat-rate pricing and 15-year track record
4Build dedicated landing pages (not homepage) for each practice area
5For Meta: set up retargeting pixel, build lookalike audiences from existing clients
6Target corporate HR directors and in-house counsel on Meta — not general consumers
7Weekly: refresh ad creative, review search terms, adjust bids, monitor competitor ads
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hours 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hours/week FOREVER
