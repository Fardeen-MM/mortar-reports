4Coordinate partner engagement — comment/share each other's content

02
Get Found — Paid Search & Targeted Ads
The Problem: Zero paid ads across 25+ markets. When GCs search, PierFerd doesn't exist.
🛠️ DIY Fix
1Create Google Ads account, set up conversion tracking
2Research keywords by practice area + city for each market
3Build campaigns per practice group (IP, corporate, litigation, employment)
4Create dedicated landing pages (not homepage) for each practice area
5Set up negative keywords, geo-targeting, bid strategy
6Write 3-5 ad variations per group, refresh weekly
7Review search terms and performance weekly, optimize bids
⏱️ Setup: 20-30 hours (per market cluster) 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hours/week FOREVER ⚠️ With 25+ markets, this is a full-time job.
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research across all 25+ markets (1,000+ terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture by practice area and geography
  • Ad copywriting — 3-5 variations per ad group
  • Landing page design and conversion optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup and monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments and budget optimization (weekly)
  • Fresh ad creative every week (platforms reward this)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, and landing pages
  • Competitor ad monitoring across all markets
  • Monthly performance reporting by practice area
