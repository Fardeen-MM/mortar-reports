The Playbook

How to Close Each Gap (Step by Step)

Here's exactly how to fix each gap — no gatekeeping. Fair warning: marketing isn't a one-time project. It's ongoing. These steps don't stop.

01
Get Found — Paid Advertising
The Problem: Zero paid visibility for family law, probate, and trust practice areas.
🛠️ DIY Fix
Setup (One-Time):
1 Create Google Ads account, set up conversion tracking
2 Research keywords: "divorce attorney Miami," "probate lawyer Brickell," etc.
3 Build 3-5 ad groups organized by practice area + intent
4 Create dedicated landing pages (not your homepage)
5 Set up Meta Business Manager, install Pixel, build audiences
Weekly (Forever):
6 Review search terms, add negative keywords
7 Create fresh ad creative (platforms penalize stale ads)
8 Monitor competitor ads, adjust bids and budgets
9 A/B test headlines, CTAs, landing page elements
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hours 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hours/week FOREVER
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed for Miami legal)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting — 3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization (weekly)
  • Fresh ad creative every week
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews
You approve the budget and take the consultations. That's it.

Weekly (Forever):

⏱️ Setup: 8-12 hours 🔄 Ongoing: 2-4 hours/week FOREVER + retraining cycles
