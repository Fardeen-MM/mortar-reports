Scale Your Ads — Google + Meta

3
Create dedicated landing pages per service (not your homepage)
4
Set up proper conversion tracking — calls, form fills, booked consultations
5
Research and add negative keywords weekly to block irrelevant searches
6
Create fresh ad creative every week — platforms reward new content
7
Build Meta lookalike audiences from your best clients
8
A/B test headlines, CTAs, and images continuously
