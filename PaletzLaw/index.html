01
Get Found — Paid Advertising
The Problem: When landlords Google "eviction attorney Michigan," Paletz is invisible. Competitors are there.
🛠️ DIY Fix
1
Create Google Ads account, set up conversion tracking
2
Research keywords: "eviction attorney Michigan," "landlord lawyer Detroit," "tenant eviction Troy MI"
3
Build 3-5 ad groups organized by intent (eviction, lease review, fair housing)
4
Write 3 ad variations per group with dedicated landing pages
5
Set up Meta Business Manager, install Pixel, build landlord/PM audiences
6
Weekly: review search terms, pause underperformers, create fresh ad creative
7
Monthly: analyze which keywords converted to actual clients, adjust bids
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hours 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hours/week FOREVER ⚠️ Ad creative dies fast — needs refreshing every 7-14 days
✨ We Handle All of This:
  • Keyword research (500+ landlord-specific terms analyzed)
  • Campaign architecture & ad group structure
  • Ad copywriting — 3-5 variations per group
  • Landing page design & optimization
  • Conversion tracking setup & monitoring
  • Negative keyword management (weekly)
  • Bid adjustments & budget optimization (weekly)
  • Fresh ad creative (weekly — platforms reward this)
  • A/B testing headlines, CTAs, images
  • Competitor ad monitoring
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Quarterly strategy reviews
