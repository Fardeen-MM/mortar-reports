01
Get Found — Paid Advertising
You're invisible when employees search for representation at night.
🛠️ DIY Fix
1Create Google Ads account, set up conversion tracking
2Research keywords: "wrongful termination lawyer Boston," "employment discrimination attorney Cambridge," etc.
3Build 3-5 ad groups by case type (discrimination, harassment, wage theft)
4Write 3 ad variations per group with specific CTAs
5Create dedicated landing pages — not your homepage
6Set up Meta Business Manager, pixel, and audience targeting
7WEEKLY: Refresh ad creative, review search terms, add negatives, adjust bids
8MONTHLY: Analyze which keywords turned into signed clients, not just leads
⏱️ Setup: 15-25 hours 🔄 Ongoing: 8-12 hours/week FOREVER ⚠️ Ad creative dies fast. Fresh creative needed EVERY week.

  • Keyword research (500+ terms analyzed)
