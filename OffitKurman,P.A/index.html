01
Get Found — Paid Search & LinkedIn Ads
The Problem: Business owners searching for succession planning, M&A, and exit planning attorneys in Philadelphia find your competitors — not you.
🛠️ DIY Fix
1
Create Google Ads account, set up conversion tracking
2
Research B2B keywords: "business succession attorney Philadelphia," "M&A lawyer Philly," "exit planning attorney PA"
3
Build LinkedIn Ads campaign targeting business owners 55+ in Philly metro with >50 employees
4
Create dedicated landing pages for each practice area (not just the firm homepage)
5
Write 3-5 ad variations per group, test weekly
6
Review search terms weekly, add negatives, pause underperformers
7
Refresh ad creative weekly — platforms penalize stale ads
⏱️ Setup: 15-20 hours 🔄 Ongoing: 6-10 hours/week FOREVER
