Setup (One-Time):
1
Audit your LinkedIn profile — headline, banner, about section, featured content
2
Define 3-4 content pillars (e.g., commercial litigation trends, Texas Business Court developments, white-collar defense insights, AI in litigation)
3
Build a target list of 200+ GCs, CLOs, and PE partners in DFW to connect with
4
Set up a content calendar with posting schedule
Weekly (Every Week, Forever):
5
Write and publish 2-3 LinkedIn posts per week (commentary on rulings, market trends, practical takeaways)
6
Engage with 15-20 target GC/CLO posts — thoughtful comments, not "great post"
7
Send 5-10 personalized connection requests to target decision-makers
8
Respond to every comment and DM within 24 hours
9
Write one longer-form article per month (JD Supra, LinkedIn Article, or firm blog)
